Adding more photos

Put your photos in the memories folder beside this page. The first ten photo slots are ready for memory-01.jpg through memory-10.jpg. To add more, add another photo file and copy one item into the memories list in this page, then change its filename and caption. The gallery has no fixed photo limit and lays out new cards automatically.
